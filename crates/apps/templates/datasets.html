The doors
  • /mcp the agent door One tool, glossql, taking statements. An agent declares, glosses and reads here; USE picks the dataset on every call, and the skills are served as resources.
  • /<dataset>/query the arrow door POST a read as the body, get an Arrow IPC stream back — no row cap, straight from the engine. pyarrow reads it directly.
  • /<dataset>/app the door for people The dataset's page is the docket: the questions the agent could not settle wait for you, with its metrics, its quality, its lineage, and every table and read as a file under Export. The apps written over the dataset sit in the bar's picker.
